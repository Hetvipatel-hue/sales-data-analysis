POWER BI QUICK BUILD GUIDE

1. Import data/sales_data.csv using Get Data > Text/CSV.
2. Rename imported table to Sales.
3. Check data types:
   - Order Date: Date
   - Quantity: Whole Number
   - Discount: Decimal Number (format as Percentage)
   - Unit Price, Sales, Cost, Profit: Decimal Number
4. Create the Date table and DAX measures in DAX_Measures.txt.
5. Create relationship Date[Date] -> Sales[Order Date].
6. Add visuals:
   - Card: Total Sales
   - Card: Total Profit
   - Card: Total Orders
   - Card: Profit Margin
   - Line chart: Date[Year-Month] and Total Sales
   - Clustered bar: Category and Total Sales
   - Bar chart: Region and Total Profit
   - Donut: Customer Segment and Total Sales
   - Slicers: Year, Region, Category, Sales Channel
7. Save as Sales_Dashboard.pbix.
8. Export a screenshot to powerbi/dashboard_screenshot.png.
